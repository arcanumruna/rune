## What was verified clean

### (1) Conservation

**Where credits are taken.** There is exactly one forward site: `handleProduce`, `ClientGateway.cpp:2176`.
- A credit is taken only on `EnqueueResult::Enqueued` and only in `shardMode_`.
- `shardMode_` implies that every worker has an `ackSink_`: `BrokerNode.cpp:3115` and `:3125` are both set in the same attach loop.
- Every produce the gateway answers itself returns before line 2176 and takes nothing: redirect, UnknownTopic/Partition, intended-role NotLeader, bootstrap NotLeader, Internal (delta, count, CRC), MessageTooLarge, marker-queued NotLeader, TooLarge, and back-pressure NotLeader.
- `onClientFrame_` is the only Produce dispatch. The shard-mode controlled poll is the only subscription poll.

**Where credits come back.** Every answer to an op-ring produce reaches `emitProduceAck_` with `gatewayCredit` set:
- `produceCore_` stamps the flag on its result shell before any exit (`Worker.h:5000`). That covers:
  - not owned / memory-backed;
  - role gate, rcGate and PSM-unproven;
  - empty batch, MessageTooLarge, schema rejection;
  - reservation exhausted;
  - handleProduceBatch errors;
  - acks=leader.
- Parked waiters (`PendingAcksAll`, `DurablePending`) carry the whole `ret`. So HW fire, timeout, §3.2 failure, fsync, `failOwnPending_` and purge all keep the flag.
- `refuseNotOwned_` sets the flag explicitly (`Worker.h:4124`).
- The flag is not encoded, and no answer is encoded and then decoded before the sink: with an `ackSink_`, answers never go to `ret_` or `retFifo_`.

**Where acks are dropped.** The sink drops acks in three cases:
- the session is closed;
- the session is `PubFailed` (the Failed signal reaches the gateway's `closeSession_`);
- an orphan, whose session is unknown after the I1 lifecycle position.

All three imply the gateway session is closed. Its budget dies in `closeSession_`, which is the only `sessions_.remove`. Session ids are monotonic (`SessionTable::nextSessionId`), so a late Credit can never land on another session.

**Lost.** Acks of a Lost session stay queued. The session is reaped on the Lost lease.

**acks=0.** The worker always answers, and an `AckEntry` has no acks field, so the ack is always claimed.

**Client retransmits and duplicates.** Each forwarded frame gets exactly one answer: a duplicate gets its original offsets.

**Ownership moves mid-flight.** A moved produce is answered by `refuseNotOwned_` or by the purge, both with credit.

**Over-forwarding.** The gateway cannot forward past the budget:
- Admission checks the same sid as the credit. The header sid at bytes 8..15 is the session `Produce::decode` uses.
- It runs synchronously in the same poll.
- So `produceInFlight <= budget` always holds, which keeps `creditBlockedSessions_` exact.

**Double return.** None:
- `noteCredit_` runs only on `pos > 0`.
- `e.credits` is zeroed only after `tryReport` succeeds.
- The `creditDirty_` list holds at most one entry per live session, and its compaction is correct.
- Slot reuse resets `Edges`.
- `erase_`'s backward shift moves `Edges` along with its key; the list stores sids, not indices.

### (2) Deadlock

A credit comes back only through a claim on the worker's own publication. That needs nothing from the paused session's gateway image.

- A refused report keeps its count and is retried every pass. `reportSessionSignals_` runs unconditionally at the end of `doWorkShard_`.
- Signals are written only when `retFifo_` is empty, so they never enter the FIFO. The F15 §6 FIFO bound is unaffected.
- No parked answer waits on a client frame:
  - acks=all waits on the HW, bounded by the ack timeout;
  - Strict waits on io completion;
  - a duplicate waits on its original;
  - rc and PSM builds answer NotLeader at once.

### (4) Leases and close

- `reapExpired` precedence is lost > paused > normal, and is correct.
- When a session is Held, it stays paused even with credits left. Credits returned while Held are reused only after Released.
- `closeSession_` decrements the blocked count before `remove`.

### (5) Hot path

- Gateway: one increment and compare per forwarded produce.
- Worker: one `a.credit` branch per claim.
- No atomics, allocation or virtual calls. Uses existing tables only; `Edges` grows by 5 B.

## Findings

### MAJOR-1: the per-session budget does not bound one worker's ack queue; about 15 stalled gateway sessions, which one process can open, still starve the whole shard for up to 10 minutes

**Where:**
- `ClientGateway.h:146-147` (budget 1024, max 4096)
- `Worker.h:1848` (`room <= 0`: op drain skipped)
- `Worker.h:4847` and `:4853` (direct-ingress poll budget `= ackRoom_()`)

**Why it happens:**
- Each non-reading gateway session ends up holding about one budget of queued, unclaimable acks on the worker. Its window's claims return credits, so the gateway refills it until the window is full; after that, a full budget sits held. The new integration test shows exactly this: `inFlight == sessionAcksPending`.
- `ackRoom_() = freeAckSlots − owed`. So 16 × 1024 = 16384 = `kAckCapacity` makes room ≤ 0 even with no owed returns. With parked acks=all or Strict owed returns, it takes 15 or fewer sessions.
- At `RUNE_GATEWAY_PRODUCE_CREDITS=4096`, the configured maximum that the `static_assert` blesses, it takes 4 sessions.
- The header comment ("16 fully-stalled gateway sessions fit beside the per-pass intake reserve") is arithmetically wrong. 16 budgets *are* the whole queue, with no reserve left.

**Failure scenario:**
1. One endpoint-unaware client process, or 16 slow consumers, opens 16 sessions on the gateway and stops reading acks after a burst.
2. Worker W's ack queue fills with their held acks.
3. `drainIngress_` returns 0 every pass (`ackRoomOpPauses_` climbs). Every op-ring op stalls: every other session's gateway-routed produces, PushRead and Subscribe, and role and ownership ops.
4. `ingressPollBudget_()` is 0, so W's endpoint-aware direct-ingress clients (the fast path) stop too.
5. Healthy gateway sessions' forwarded produces sit unexecuted, so their credits never return and they are credit-paused as well.
6. Nothing clears it until the stalled sessions are reaped. That happens after `maxHeldIdleNs_` (10 minutes) without Progress, or on the Lost lease if they die.

This is the "slow client starves the shard" failure D8c set out to remove, with the threshold raised from 1 session to about 15. No test covers N > 1 stalled sessions.

**Fix direction (pick one):**
- **(a) Gateway-side per-worker aggregate.** Track routed in-flight per (worker) as well as per session. Keep the sum for worker w at or below `kAckCapacity − (kMaxOwedReturnsCeiling + kMaxIngressDrainLimit + kMaxFragmentLimit)`, and pause the session whose forward would exceed it. Credits already arrive per worker (`ret.partition = workerId`), so the gateway can attribute them.
- **(b) Shrink the per-session budget with the number of routed sessions.** Use `min(budget, reserveFreeRoom / activeRoutedSessions)`.

Either way, add a regression with `kAckCapacity / budget + 1` stalled sessions that asserts `ackRoomOpPauses == 0` and that a direct-ingress produce on the same worker is still acked.

### MEDIUM-1: no test covers credit return on the produce refusal, timeout and purge paths, which are the leak paths

**Where:**
- `Worker.h:4112-4129` (`refuseNotOwned_` builds a *fresh* `WorkerReturn`)
- `Worker.h:5000` (shell stamp)
- `GatewayProduceCreditTest.cpp` and `ShardWorkerAckTest.cpp` (only the success paths: acks=0 and acks=all with ISR = {self})

**The gap:** Today every answer path carries the flag; I traced them all. But conservation depends on each answer site remembering a worker-local, unencoded bool. `refuseNotOwned_` already shows the hazard: it builds its own struct, and the next such site that forgets the flag leaks one credit per answer.

**Failure scenario (regression):** a future answer site leaks. During ownership churn or a leader move, a session takes 1024 NotLeader answers. From then on it is permanently credit-paused. No Held, no Lost, no credit ever returns. It is silently reaped after 10 minutes. No test would catch it.

**Fix:**
- Add `*Regression*` cases that assert `produceInFlight` returns to 0 after each of these:
  - a stale-incarnation op (`refuseNotOwned_`);
  - the role-gate NotLeader (BecomeFollower applied);
  - the acks=all ack timeout;
  - the ownership purge of parked acks=all;
  - a Strict fsync completion;
  - the reservation-exhausted NotLeader.
- Optionally, derive `gatewayCredit` in one place: `executeFromWire_` stamps it for `Type::Produce`, or the `AckEntry` gets it from a "came from op ring" bit set at decode. Then a new answer site cannot forget it.

### MEDIUM-2: no evidence for gateway-path throughput under the new pause

**Where:** report §"Produce sanity"; `ClientGateway.cpp:1010-1018` and `:2176`.

**The gap:**
- The only bench (`tp.sh`) is endpoint-aware. The report itself says the only change on its path is one sink branch, so the code this diff actually changes for throughput was never measured.
- The gateway-routed path now adds one Credit `ret_` message per session per worker pass beside the per-ack notifications.
- It can also pause a healthy acks=all client whenever 1024 produces are outstanding. With the reported p99 of 10–31 ms, that caps a session at roughly 33–100k batches/s during tail events.

**Failure scenario:** an endpoint-unaware or fallback client is throttled, or the extra `ret_` traffic pushes `retFifo_` into high-water pauses. Neither would be visible in any current number.

**Fix:** one interleaved ABAB gateway-routed run (endpoint-unaware client, 3-node acks=all, 1 KiB, MTU 1408 + GSO). Record `creditProduceAborts` and `retFifoHighWaterPauses` in the perf findings spec.

### LOW-1: every Credit refreshes the lease, not only leaving the pause

**Where:** `ClientGateway.cpp:1046`, which runs unconditionally before the `wasBlocked` check.

**The gap:** The report and the code comment say "leaving the credit pause refreshes `lastSeenNs`". In fact any Credit does, so worker claims count as client liveness for unpaused sessions too. The effect is bounded (claims to a dead client stop at its receive window or at NOT_CONNECTED), so nothing breaks.

**Fix:** either guard the refresh with `wasBlocked`, or fix the comment and the report.

### LOW-2: `setSessionProduceCredits` after sessions exist corrupts `creditBlockedSessions_`

**Where:** `ClientGateway.h:150`.

**Failure scenario:** the budget is lowered while a session holds more in flight than the new budget. That session is never counted as blocked. If no other session is paused, the `(held | blocked) == 0` fast path admits its frames, and forwarding is unbounded. Raising the budget makes `--creditBlockedSessions_` wrap the `size_t`, so every frame takes the slow path forever. Only a comment guards it; today only BrokerNode calls it, at construction.

**Fix:** `assert(sessions_.size() == 0)`, or recompute the count in the setter.

### LOW-3: any session at budget pushes every frame on every image through the slow path

**Where:** `ClientGateway.cpp:1016`.

**The gap:** A healthy high-rate acks=all client sits at 1024 in flight often. While it does, each client frame on the gateway decodes its templateId and does a `sessions_.find`, and Produce frames then do a second find in `handleProduce`. This is cheap, but it turns the D8b "nothing paused" fast path into the common case only when no one is at budget.

**Fix:** acceptable as is; note it. Or check only the frame's own session, using a per-image cache of the paused sid.

### LOW-4: the budget is per session, so one slow partition pauses the session's whole image

**The gap:** One acks=all partition parked during an ISR-shrink window (up to `replicaLagMax` or the ack timeout) can use up the 1024 credits. The aborted Produce then blocks that session's produces to healthy partitions, and also the Metadata request it would need to re-route, and its Keepalives. This is Kafka-like (in-flight per connection), so it is acceptable.

**Fix:** document it next to the Held/ABORT note in `doWork`.

### LOW-5: leftovers (acknowledged in the report)

- The two D8b real-driver tests still run on the legacy transport, and their comments still say the pause "can lag (D8c)".
- `heldProduceAbortsForTest` changed meaning: it now counts every paused-produce ABORT, Held or no credit. Any external metric or dashboard that reads it should be renamed or relabelled.
- The `ShardWorkerAckRegression` comment says "acks=all (op deferAck unset: answered at append)", so that test never parks. Only the integration test covers a parked acks=all, and only with ISR = {self}.

## (6) Tests

- **The new deterministic tests are well built.** `GatewayProduceCreditRegression.*` drives the real `admitClientFrame_` and `onClientFrame_` against a real worker, sink and driver. They would fail without the credit take and report: the first test asserts `consumed <= C` in one poll and `ackRoomOpPauses == 0`.

## Verified clean

### Conservation of `routedInFlight_`

- **The worker is attributed consistently.**
  - The gateway counts on `ownerWorkerId` = `router_.workerForPartition`, the same arithmetic `enqueueOp` routes by.
  - The router is fixed (F7), so an ownership or role move never changes the worker; refusal and purge happen on the same worker.
  - Credit carries `r.partition = workerId_`, and `workerId_` is the pool index (`WorkerPool.h:97`).
- **Taken only on `Enqueued`.** TooLarge, back-pressure NotLeader, marker-queued NotLeader and every pre-enqueue answer take nothing. A deferred frame takes nothing.
- **Returned exactly once.**
  - Each claimed routed ack returns exactly one Credit, attributed to its worker.
  - `closeSession_` subtracts the session's per-worker shares once, and the session is removed in the same call.
  - Late Credits for a closed sid hit `if (!s) return`. Sids are monotonic, so no double decrement.
  - A refused announce halts the worker, so no silent orphan.
  - Dropped acks (close, PubFailed, orphan) all imply a gateway close.
  - A worker halt ends the process.
- **No underflow path.** The clamp is per worker.
- **`setSessionProduceCredits` (LOW-2)** recounts correctly and does not touch the per-worker counters.

### Worker throttle

- The worker has **one** physical throttle, `ackRoom_()`, and the round does not change it. `A + O <= kAckCapacity` still holds for any client behaviour, so no enqueue can fail and the halt stays unreachable.
- The two "halves" exist only as gateway accounting.
- Only produce answers enter the ack queue: `emitProduceAck_` is the sole `enqueue`. Role, replication and PushRead ops do not take ack slots (held PushReads only reduce room through `O`, which is conservative). So no other op kind can overflow either share.
- Parked routed acks=all count both in the gateway cap and in `O`, which is conservative.

### Fairness

- A live but busy session that is heavy in the band still progresses. Its claims lower `produceInFlightOn[w]`, and below 64 it is admitted again. Inside the band it is throttled to 64 in flight on that worker, never starved.
- Light sessions' in-flight is transient, so they alone cannot hold `used >= 12288`.

### ABORT without consume

- The cap returns `false` only in `shardMode_`, and only the controlled poll runs in shard mode (`ClientGateway.cpp:463`). The uncontrolled `onFragment`, which discards the result, never sees `false`.
- An aborted first fragment yields 0 fragments, so the duty cycle idles and backs off. That is not a busy loop.
- ABORT is per image, so other clients keep polling.

### Memory and hot path

- `SessionAckSink::acks_` is a `std::array` inside the sink object. That object is `make_unique`d once in `setShardClient`, so the 1.25 MiB is never allocated on the hot path and never on a stack.
- Per routed produce, the gateway adds one load and compare (`workerAdmitsProduce_`) and two increments. No atomics, allocation or virtual calls.

## Findings

### MEDIUM-R1: about 76 stalled sessions from one process (67 at the maximum budget) still fill a worker's routed cap; every gateway-routed produce to that worker is then deferred for up to 10 minutes

**Where:**
- `ClientGateway.cpp:1057` (`workerAdmitsProduce_`)
- `ClientGateway.h:133` (`kDefaultMaxHeldIdleNs` = 600 s)

**Why it happens:**
- Below the congested band, every stalled session is admitted up to its whole budget, so 12 stalled sessions at 1024 reach 12288.
- Inside the band, a newly stalled session counts as "light" until it holds 64 unclaimed acks. That happens once its egress window is full: claims return credits until then.
- So 64 more stalled sessions × 64 = 4096 fill the band to the hard cap. At `RUNE_GATEWAY_PRODUCE_CREDITS=4096` it takes 3 + 64 sessions.
- The session cap is 4095, so one process can open them.

**Failure scenario:**
1. A misbehaving endpoint-unaware process opens 76 sessions to partitions on worker W, bursts, and stops reading.
2. `routedInFlight_[W]` sits at 16384.
3. Every gateway-routed Produce to W, from every healthy fallback client, is deferred.
4. Each healthy client's image is then stalled behind that frame, including its produces to other workers, its Metadata and its Keepalives.
5. This lasts until the stalled sessions are reaped: `maxHeldIdleNs` (10 min) if they stay connected, or the Lost lease if they die. Re-opening repeats it.

**What remains protected:** W's direct clients, op ring, and role and replication ops. That is why this is MEDIUM, not MAJOR.

**Overstated claims:** the report's line "up to 64 light sessions … keep being served" holds only while the band is not itself filled by stalled-light sessions. The 20-session test stays below that threshold: it ends with 3 × 4096 + 17 × 64 = 13376 in flight.

**Fix direction (pick one):**
- **Event-based eviction (time-independent).** When the hard cap defers a frame, close the Held session with the largest share on W that has had no Progress since the cap was last reached.
- **Or ticket it.** Ticket it explicitly against G-A6 and do not call G-A6 closed for the gateway path.

**Regression either way:** `(cap − band) / budget + band / 64 + 1` stalled sessions, then assert that a healthy new session's produce to W is acked.

### LOW-R1: a close frees the cap share before the worker drops that session's queued acks

**Where:** `ClientGateway.cpp:989`; `Worker.h:1988` (the sink drops orphaned acks lazily, at flush).

**The gap:**
- The invariant comment at `Worker.h:229` (`A + O <= kRoutedAckBudget + owed + direct`) is not strict.
- Example: a reap of 16 sessions frees 16384 of cap at once, while up to 16384 of their acks are still queued on W until its close op and the next flush.
- Physically this is safe: `ackRoom_` bounds every drain. At worst there is one op-drain pass with no room (`ackRoomOpPauses_`).

**Fix:** reword the comment to "after the worker has applied every close".

### LOW-R2: the worker's "reserve" is not reserved against direct clients or held PushReads

**The gap:** Stalled *direct* (worker-ingress) sessions can still drive `ackRoom_` to 0. That stalls W's op drain, including the routed share the gateway thinks is available. The report already lists this.

**Fix:** it needs its own ticket before G-A6 is claimed on any path.

### LOW-R3: per-session and per-frame cost of the per-worker split

**Where:** `SessionTable.h:58`.

**The gap:**
- `produceInFlightOn` adds 256 B per Session: +1 MiB across a full 4096-slot table, larger `erase_` shifts, and a second cache line touched per routed produce.
- `uint16_t` would suffice, since a session holds at most 4096.
- While a frame is cap-deferred, every duty cycle re-runs `Produce::decode`, `sessions_.find`, the registry lookups and the leader checks up to the cap check. Idle backoff bounds it.

**Fix:** optional.

### LOW-R4: cap deferral is head-of-line across workers, and a deferred frame refreshes the lease

**Where:** `ClientGateway.cpp:1738` (F23 refresh before the cap check at `:1942`).

**The gap:**
- A frame deferred for W blocks that session's produces to other workers, plus its Metadata and Keepalive. The LOW-4 comment documents only the per-session-budget case.
- Every redelivery of the deferred frame refreshes `lastSeenNs`. This is harmless, because only a live sender's image redelivers.

**Fix:** extend the LOW-4 comment.

### LOW-R5: no multi-worker test of the per-worker counters

**The gap:** `GatewayProduceCreditTest`'s `Rig` has one worker. Nothing tests a Credit naming worker ≠ 0, or a close returning shares on several workers at once. A wrong attribution would only show up as `produceCreditUnderflows` in production.

**Fix:** add a two-worker case: forward to both, Credit one, close, and assert every `routedInFlight_[w]` is 0.
