## MAJOR

### MAJOR-1: A redirected JoinGroup or SyncGroup is not re-sent on the new session; the member stalls for `sessionTimeoutMs` (30 s default)

**Where:**
- Client `src/group/GroupConsumerHub.cpp:1086-1100` (`onConnected` re-sends only from `Unjoined` / `Rejoining`).
- `:672-687` (in `Joining`, it re-sends only once `joinDeadline` has passed).
- `:375` (`joinDeadline = now + sessionTimeoutMs`).
- `include/rune/client/group/GroupConsumer.h:117` (`sessionTimeoutMs = 30000`).
- Broker `libs/gateway/src/ClientGateway.cpp:5375,5430-5449`.

**Scenario (a member bootstrapped on a follower, which is the exact D4 repro):**
1. The member connects to follower F. `onConnected` sends JoinGroup and the consumer enters `Joining`.
2. F now answers `ErrorResponse{Redirect, L}`.
3. `Client.cpp:1828-1832` sets `haveWork` (`groupActive`). The handler finds no produce partition for corrId 0, so it
   does a seed `retarget(L)`, sets `redirectPending`, and calls `submitReconnect()`.
4. On `ConnectResponse{OK}`, `Client.cpp:1755-1761` calls `groupConsumerHub_.onConnected(newSid)`.
5. The consumer is still in `Joining`, so **nothing is sent**.
6. Nothing else ever answers the old JoinGroup: the Redirect `break`s before the generic error path, and the hub
   never sees the ErrorResponse.
7. The member therefore idles until `joinDeadline`, 30 s after the original send. Only then does `doWork` re-send
   JoinGroup on the leader session.

The same happens to `Syncing`: a SyncGroup redirected after a coordinator move waits out `joinDeadline`, then rejoins.
It also happens on every coordinator failover that lands a joining member on a follower.

This is a liveness and latency regression against the D4 claim that the member "re-joins there". The dockerized test
passes only because it allows 300 s and does not bound the time to assignment (see MEDIUM-2).

Before D4 the member never converged at all. So this is not a regression against HEAD, but the fix is incomplete.

**Suggested fix (client, cold path):**
- In `GroupConsumerHub::onConnected`, a NEW session means any in-flight Join or Sync on the old session can never be
  answered. Treat `Joining` and `Syncing` like `Rejoining`: call `sendJoinGroup(*impl, sessionId, sendFn)`. Kafka
  likewise re-sends JoinGroup after a coordinator change.
- Alternatively, in the Client Redirect handler, when `groupActive`, mark every group consumer in `Joining` or
  `Syncing` as `Rejoining` before `submitReconnect()`.
- Add a client-side test: a fake follower redirects the first JoinGroup, and the test asserts that the JoinGroup on
  the new session goes out within one duty cycle, not after `sessionTimeoutMs`.
- Tighten the dr-e2e test to assert assignment within a few seconds.

## MEDIUM

### MEDIUM-1: The split-vote mitigation has no discriminating test in the default run; the jitter sweep is not wired into ctest

**Where:**
- `libs/consensus/src/Election.cpp:1421-1435`.
- `libs/consensus/tests/consensus/ElectionLogTest.cpp:2973-2974` (`SIM_JITTER` read from the environment only).
- `libs/consensus/CMakeLists.txt` (no `SIM_JITTER` test registration).

**Failure scenario:**
- Deleting the whole mitigation block leaves `rune_consensus_tests` at 444/444 GREEN at the default `SIM_JITTER=0`,
  which is how CI and agents run it. It is also GREEN at 10 ms.
- `D29bRegression_ElectionsConvergeUnderLatencyJitterAndStallsRegression` passes without the mitigation. Its 2000 ms
  bound is far above the measured worst case of 878 ms.
- The 200-seed lost-round and worst-case harness behind the spec table is not committed. The numbers in
  `specs/2026-10-08-prevote-design.md` ("13→1 … 751→257 ms") cannot be reproduced from the repo.
- A future edit can therefore silently undo the mitigation, which breaks the "every fix ships a RED-first regression"
  rule.

**Suggested fix:**
1. Add a deterministic unit test. A NOMINATE node with an active, unwon pre-ballot receives a grantable
   `onPreVoteRequest`. Assert that its pre-ballot is cleared, that `nominationDeadlineNs_ >= now + electionTimeout/2`,
   and that a won pre-ballot is NOT cleared.
2. Commit the measurement as a test with a threshold, for example "5-node hand-off, 200 seeds, 5 ms jitter: lost
   rounds ≤ 20". Mark it `*Regression*`, and pin the jitter in the test itself (as the jitter test already does) so
   it needs no environment variable.
3. Register a second ctest for the sims with `ENVIRONMENT SIM_JITTER=5`, using `set_tests_properties`, or loop jitter
   values inside the sim tests.

### MEDIUM-2: The dockerized D4 test cannot catch MAJOR-1, has a stale docstring and leftover diagnostics, and has no in-process counterpart

**Where:** `dr-e2e/tests/test_cg_rebalance.py:249-289`, and the absence of any client-to-gateway integration test.

**Problems:**
- The docstring still describes the old mechanism: "NotCoordinator -> rediscover".
- The `# DIAG-D4 (temporary)` per-member dump of 3000 characters ships in the test.
- Correctness assertions only: single owner, no committed loss, bounded duplicates. With `timeout_s=300`, a 30 s join
  stall passes.
- The only D4 test below Docker is `CoordinatorRedirectTest`. It is gateway-only: it proves the ErrorResponse is
  emitted, not that the client follows it. That conflicts with the integration-before-E2E gate. The one thing that
  was broken here was the convergence across client and broker, and no fast test covers it.

**Suggested fix:**
- Fix the docstring and remove the DIAG print.
- Assert time-to-first-assignment, for example under 5 s after the members start.
- Add an in-process integration test that drives a real Client against two gateways (follower and leader) through the
  existing invoker or redirect test rigs. Assert: JoinGroup on the follower → Redirect → JoinGroup on the leader
  session → Stable. Do the same for `InitProducerId` with a txn producer.

### MEDIUM-3 (latent): Leader-state CheckQuorum assumes followers send AppendPosition within H in every follower election state

**Where:** `libs/consensus/src/Election.cpp:536-540, 621-625, 1327-1332`, and the follower pass-through states at
`:663-731` (`PHASE-2-ARCHIVE-DEFERRED`).

**Background:**
- `hasActiveFollowerQuorum_` counts a follower only through `timeOfLastAppendPositionNs`.
- Today every follower state is a pass-through: NLT, then Closed in the same pass, and `FollowerLiveness` then sends
  AppendPosition on the status interval. So a live quorum always refreshes within H, and there is no false trip today.

**Latent failure:** this breaks once archive catch-up or log replication becomes real.
- A follower in `FollowerCatchup` or `FollowerLogReplication` can take longer than H. Aeron's leader states never
  time out, so Aeron tolerates that.
- If those states do not publish AppendPosition on an interval, the leader-elect stands down after H on every attempt.
- The next leader also needs that quorum to catch up, so the cluster livelocks and never completes an election.

**Suggested fix:**
- Pin the contract now. Add a comment at `leaderStatesTimedOut_` and at each `PHASE-2-ARCHIVE-DEFERRED` assert:
  "follower states must publish AppendPosition every status interval (Aeron updateFollowerLogReplication does)".
- Add a sim test in which a follower is held in a non-Closed follower state longer than H while it keeps sending
  AppendPosition, and the leader-elect does not stand down. This keeps the "time-independent" claim in the comment
  true by construction rather than by today's pass-through.

## LOW

### LOW-1: The mitigation re-defers on every re-asked pre-vote

**Where:** `Election.cpp:1427-1434`.

The candidate re-sends `PreVoteRequest` every status interval to members whose grant it has not received
(`sendPreVoteRequests_`). If the grant response is lost, or the link is one-way, each re-ask pushes the granter's
`nominationDeadlineNs_` forward again by U[T/2,T), for as long as the requester keeps pre-balloting.

The reviewer probe (Verification above) found no starvation, and the mitigation still improves the worst case there.
The deferral is non-binding and the requester's cycles are random.

Optional hardening: defer at most once per `(candidateId, term)`. Remember the last deferred pair and skip the push
when it repeats.

### LOW-2: The leader-state StandDown is sent once, and only Closed followers drop the lease on it

**Where:** `Election.cpp:1335-1341` and `1205-1207` (`onStandDown` returns unless `Closed`).

- A lost StandDown leaves that follower waiting out `FollowerLiveness` (H).
- A lease holder that is not Closed keeps Z's lease for up to H after the last NLT.

Today follower states are pass-through, so this is bounded by H, the same ruling as r2 LOW-1. `resign()` re-sends its
StandDown from Canvass on the interval; the leader-state path does not. The spec line "StandDown drops the peers'
lease at once" holds only for Closed followers. Either re-send the StandDown on the status interval while canvassing
after a CheckQuorum stand-down, as `resigned_` does, or reword the spec.

### LOW-3: A denied pre-ballot still reports a future `nextDeadlineNs`

**Where:** `Election.cpp:172-176`.

The r1 LOW-2 fix makes a WON pre-ballot due at once. A DENIED one (`preBallot_.denied`, set in `onPreVoteResponse`)
is acted on at the next `doWork`, which goes to Canvass immediately. But `nextDeadlineNs` still reports
`preBallot_.deadlineNs`, up to T away, so a deadline-sleeping invoker delays the canvass by up to T. The only caller is
test code, which is why this is LOW.

Fix: `preBallot_.active && !preBallot_.denied && !preBallotWon_() ? preBallot_.deadlineNs : …`.

### LOW-4: The self pre-vote bit is only ever cleared

**Where:** `Election.cpp:412-414`.

If the gate re-admits us later in the same pre-ballot, the bit is never set again. That round then needs one extra
peer grant and may be lost needlessly. The cost is one round and there is no safety impact.

Fix: recompute the bit with `if (mayVoteFor(self)) markPreVote_(self); else clear`.

### LOW-5: The jitter and stall convergence test does not discriminate

**Where:** `ElectionLogTest.cpp:3404-3443`.

The `EXPECT_LE(tookMs, 2'000)` bound is more than twice the measured worst case. The test passes without the
mitigation and would pass with substantial regressions. Either tighten the bound, for example to H + 4 rounds ≈
750 ms if that is stable across seeds, or relabel the test as a smoke test rather than a `*Regression*`.

### LOW-6: The `D4TxnRegression.*` tests are pin tests, not regressions

**Where:** `libs/node/tests/integration/F15EosRegressionTest.cpp:5826-5974`.

- They pin behaviour for bugs that were never reproduced, so none was RED-first. The `Regression` suffix pulls them
  into the regression suite under a misleading name. Consider `D4TxnPin.*`, or note in the header that they are pins.
- `MalformedEndTxnFramesAreIgnoredRegression` asserts almost nothing: the truncation loop discards every result and
  ends with `SUCCEED()`. Its value depends on running under ASan or UBSan, which build-fast does not use. Assert the
  return value on each truncated frame (`== 0`, nothing encoded) so the test checks something without a sanitizer.

### LOW-7: `CoordinatorRedirectTest` covers one group template and one txn template

**Where:** `libs/gateway/tests/gateway/CoordinatorRedirectTest.cpp:79-119`.

The redirect runs before the per-template dispatch, so coverage is adequate today. A refactor that moves the call into
individual `case`s would go unnoticed for Heartbeat, OffsetCommit, OffsetFetch, SyncGroup, LeaveGroup,
InitProducerId, AddPartitions, AddOffsets and TxnOffsetCommit. A table-driven loop over all eleven templates is cheap.

### LOW-8: Coordinator Redirects have no client-side budget or loop guard

**Where:** client `Client.cpp:1802-1986`.

- Each coordinator Redirect does `retarget` + `submitReconnect`. Data-op redirects are bounded per corrId
  (`kMaxRedirects`); group and txn redirects carry corrId 0 or a txn corr, and nothing bounds them.
- With transiently inconsistent leader views (F → L, and L, already deposed, → F), or a follower whose sticky view
  still names a just-died leader for up to H, every group or txn round costs a full reconnect. In the dead-leader case
  that runs through the same connect-retry and backoff path that data-op redirects already take.
- It converges once views converge, which is why this is LOW. A cheap guard: if a coordinator Redirect names the
  endpoint we were redirected away from within the last H, arm a metadata refresh and back off instead of
  retargeting.

### LOW-9 (unverified): Under `multiNodeRouting`, a coordinator Redirect moves the seed session

**Where:** client `Client.cpp:1888-1918` (the per-partition branch needs `havePart`, which is never true for group or
txn corrIds) and `:1928-1959`.

A coordinator Redirect falls through to the single-transport seed retarget. That is the intended move: the
coordinator is the consensus leader. But when the seed node is also a partition leader (Milestone D distributed
leadership), its in-flight produces on the seed session are resent only if they were marked redirected; the rest rely
on per-attempt deadlines. That is a one-time churn per client. Worth one multi-node client test once distributed
leadership is live.

## Checked and found sound

- **Consensus safety.**
  - CheckQuorum only leaves leader states. It sends StandDown at its own `leadershipTermId_`, which only Closed
    followers of that leader and term act on.
  - It writes no durable state. The TermLog entry for T from `leaderInit` stays, and the next election is at T+1 or
    higher.
  - The mitigation and the pre-ballot changes are non-binding: no term, floor or vote is written. The LOW-3 restart
    uses `candidateTermId_ + 1` only for the non-binding pre-ballot.
  - No path admits two leaders in a term, and no term regresses.
- **CheckQuorum grace.** `leaderSinceNs_` is set on entering `LeaderLogReplication`, so the grace runs from the first
  leader state. Member clocks are not cleared by `reset()` (Aeron-faithful); that only makes the check more lenient.
- **Mutual deferral.** Two simultaneous pre-ballots that grant each other both abandon and re-arm at independent
  random times in [T/2, T). That is probabilistic like Aeron's [0, T/2) back-off, with no deterministic livelock.
  A won pre-ballot is never abandoned.
- **Self-redirect.** `BrokerNode` sets `isLeader` as `leaderId == self`, and `leaderClientEndpointFor_` returns `""`
  for the leader, so a node never redirects to itself. An unknown leader falls through to NotCoordinator, which is
  retriable.
- **Txn producer convergence.** On a Redirect the seed session changes. `TransactionalProducerImpl::doWork:841-851`
  re-sends every `InFlight` request and every produce batch on the new session immediately.
- **Client paths that relied on NotCoordinator.**
  - The group hub (`:871`, `:961`) and the txn `Rediscover` verdict (`TransactionalProducerImpl.cpp:514`) are still
    reached when the leader endpoint is unknown, or when the leader's service is not yet Loaded.
  - No client path needed NotCoordinator from a follower that knows the leader.
- **Hot path.**
  - Nothing was added to the produce or fetch paths. `redirectToCoordinator_` runs only on the group and txn
    control-plane dispatch.
  - On the leader it is one `std::function` call that returns an empty string, with no allocation.
  - The consensus additions are election-only and use plain function pointers; there are no virtual calls.
- **`docker-compose.yml`.** The `rune-crypto` context matches `Dockerfile:81`. The `RUNE_PROTOCOL_DIR` default keeps
  the previous path.

## Disposition (2026-10-08, controller)

- **MAJOR-1: FIXED, and worse than reported.** The client never followed the coordinator Redirect at all. While a
  group is active, the seed session ignores every data-op Redirect (rule MEDIUM-4); the in-process Docker
  diagnostic captured that. Fixes:
  - **Broker:** answers `ErrorResponse{NotCoordinator, leader endpoint}`, which the client can tell apart from a
    data-op Redirect.
  - **Client (rune-client-cpp a0f61a1):** `Client::onCoordinatorRedirect_` retargets the seed and reconnects.
    `GroupConsumerHub::onCoordinatorMoved` moves members to Rejoining. `onConnected` re-joins Joining and Syncing
    members (this is MAJOR-1 itself).
  - **Test:** `GroupRoutingRegression.MemberOnAFollowerJoinsTheCoordinatorRegression`, with a bound of under 5 s.
    It is RED without either half.
- **MEDIUM-1: FIXED.**
  - `D29bRegression_GrantingAPeerPreVoteDefersOurOwnCandidacy`: deterministic.
  - `D29bRegression_SplitVotesStayRareUnderJitter`: 200 seeds at 5 ms, at most 20 lost rounds and a worst case of at
    most 600 ms. Measured 12 rounds and 257 ms with the mitigation, against 35 rounds and 634 ms without it.
  - ctest `ConsensusSimsUnderJitter{5,10}Regression` re-runs the sims with `SIM_JITTER`.
  - Both new tests are RED without the mitigation.
- **MEDIUM-2: FIXED.** The docstring is corrected and the DIAG print removed. The join-latency bound lives in the
  client's in-process test (above), not in Docker.
- **MEDIUM-3: contract documented** at `leaderStatesTimedOut_`. Ruling: there is no sim test today, because the
  follower states are pass-throughs and nothing can hold a follower in a non-Closed state. The contract comment names
  the obligation for the archive work.
- **LOW-1: FIXED.** Deferral happens once per peer term (`deferredForTerm_`).
- **Ruled, not changed:**
  - LOW-2: StandDown is sent once, bounded by H.
  - LOW-3: a denied pre-ballot's `nextDeadlineNs` only causes an early wake.
  - LOW-4: the self bit is only cleared, which is conservative.
  - LOW-5: superseded by the MEDIUM-1 threshold test.
  - LOW-6: the D4Txn tests are labelled as pins of a never-reproduced report.
  - LOW-7: one template per service. The fence sits in the shared dispatch function, before the template switch.
  - LOW-8: a coordinator redirect is idempotent per episode and converges with the leader views.
  - LOW-9: to be revisited with Milestone D distributed leadership.
