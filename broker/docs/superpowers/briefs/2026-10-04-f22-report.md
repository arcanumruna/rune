## Open issues and concerns

1. **The perf runs are single runs.** Saturation of 181k is above the earlier 168–176k. DUR-1's HW = min over the ISR plus `resetFollowers` is not expected to speed anything up, so treat the difference as run-to-run spread unless an interleaved A/B shows otherwise.
2. **DUR-1 follow-ups are unchanged:**
   - the Docker DR matrix with `RUNE_SURVIVOR_LEADER_ELECTION=1` (E3);
   - the LOW-R4-3 runbook;
   - LOW-R3-2 and LOW-R3-3;
   - `DISABLED_E1b_UncleanRestartMovedBeforeFirstFetch` (controller-side KIP-966).
3. **E1d backlog is unchanged:**
   - LOW-1, 2, 3 and 5;
   - every truncation does an inline pwrite+fdatasync (acceptable on cold role-change paths);
   - the follower's acked LEO can exceed its synced LEO (follower flush is off by default). DUR-1 handles the restart by evicting the follower from the ISR, but an acked record's copy count can drop temporarily. Closing this needs the E1b/KIP-966 work.
4. **D8d backlog is unchanged:** the tombstone ring size; never-connected direct sessions are not expired before the D8b lease; there was no independent re-review of its last round.
5. **`rune_replication_integration_tests` vs the DUR-1 branch.** These 4 tests were never part of the DUR-1 review rounds. Their new expectations are the standard Kafka contract, and a reviewer may want to confirm them.
6. **The rune-protocol branch was not merged to a protocol `main`.** That step was not part of this brief, so the sibling checkout stays on `feat/self-contained-worker-shards` at dfe39be.
