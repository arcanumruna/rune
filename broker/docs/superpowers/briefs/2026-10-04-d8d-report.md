## The problem (D8c review LOW-R2), measured

Direct (worker-ingress) clients that stop reading had their acks bounded only by `ackRoom_`.

- A stalled session was paused only after its first held flush. By then it had already taken one poll's worth of frames. At the fragment-limit ceiling (`RUNE_GATEWAY_FRAGMENT_LIMIT` up to 4096, which the reserve is sized for), that is up to 4096 acks per session.
- Parked acks=all answers can also fire into the queue later.

Measured before the fix:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Real driver, worker level (10 stalled sessions) | The ack queue held **32768** (the whole queue, the gateway-routed half included). The op drain paused **2192** times. Another direct session's produce was never polled. |
| Real broker, ShardMode e2e (48 stalled sessions) | **30–32k** of the 32768 slots held. |

## Design as built

### Direct budget (`SessionAckSink`)

- **What is counted.** `admitDirect(sid)` runs per frame in the worker's existing controlled poll. It counts every direct produce from the moment it is admitted until its ack leaves the sink. That covers the owed state (parked acks=all / Strict) and the queued state.
- **Where it comes back:**
  - claimed (an OK answer or any refusal answer);
  - dropped by a fatal claim, a failed publication, or a closed / unknown session;
  - given back with `releaseDirect` for an undecodable frame.
- **How the answer carries it.** `WorkerReturn::directAck` is set on `produceCore_`'s answer shell. It is not encoded. Every answer path copies that shell, the same way `gatewayCredit` travels, and `emitProduceAck_` copies it into `AckEntry::direct`.
- **Two tags:**
  - `kDirectCounted` = counted against the worker total and the session's share.
  - `kDirectTotalOnly` = the session's announcement was not applied yet (I1), so only the worker total counts it. This keeps the per-session count exact: it can never be decremented below what was counted.
- **ABORT rules.** The frame stays on its own image and the client's offers back-pressure. A frame is ABORTed when:
  1. the session holds acks (G-A6, unchanged; folded into the same probe);
  2. the session is at its **per-session budget** (default 4096 = the owed-cap default; a test seam can change it);
  3. the worker's **direct share** is full: `Worker::kDirectAckBudget` = `kAckReserve − kMaxHeldPushReads − kMaxIngressDrainLimit` = **11264**;
  4. the share is in its last quarter and the session holds 64 or more (`kDirectLightInFlight`).
- **Why stalled sessions run out first.** Rule 4 admits only light sessions in the last quarter, the same rule as D8c's gateway band. A stalled session sits at its whole share, so it is paused first.
- **Invariant.** A static_assert checks: routed share + direct share + held-PushRead cap + one op drain ≤ `kAckCapacity`. So `ackRoom_() ≥ 4096` however many direct sessions stall (after every SessionClose has been applied, the same caveat as LOW-R1).
- **Hot-path cost:** one hash probe per direct frame (before: one branch when nothing was held), plus one decrement per claimed direct ack. No allocation, no virtual calls, no `std::function`, no atomics.

### Expiry (full share)

When the direct share is full, the worker expires one session. The policy and thrash guards are the same as D8c's `onWorkerCapFull_`:

- Only a session **held** on this worker can be chosen, and the one with the largest direct share is.
- It is never a session that had a direct ack claimed during the current episode (a slow reader).
- The worker scans only after **64 deferred passes**, and again every 64 passes.
- At most **one expiry per episode**.
- An episode ends with its expiry, or when the share drops below the band.
- The scan is cold.
- Addition over D8c: only one expiry can be in flight. A new episode does not count deferred passes until the runtime's close has erased the victim. This is needed because the gateway closes synchronously, but here the close comes back over the lifecycle ring.

How the expiry travels:

1. The worker reports `SessionSignalKind::Expire = 7` (the share goes in `baseOffset`). It is retried if the return ring is full, and dropped if the session closed first.
2. `ClientGateway::onSessionSignal_` handles it exactly like `Failed`: `closeSession_(sid, notifyExpired = true)`, so the client gets SessionExpired, every worker gets a SessionClose, a warn line is logged, and `directShareExpiries_` is counted.
3. The victim's queued acks drop at the worker's next flush, which frees its share.

**Non-idempotent duplicates on expiry:** corrected in the fix round below. As first committed (`bb1df9b`), this line said "the same trade-off as D8c". That was wrong: the expired session's whole buffered backlog was appended without being acked, so a non-idempotent client's resends duplicated all of it. Since `609ad26`, a closed session's buffered direct frames are dropped before the append, as on the gateway path. Only answers already in flight can duplicate, which is D8c's window.

### Held PushReads

- They are bounded by the static `kMaxHeldPushReads` (1024) and by the owed cap. Neither depends on what any client does: a PushRead is held only while its partition's RC state builds, and beyond the cap it is answered notReady at once.
- They do not go into the ack sink, but each one owes a return and shrinks `ackRoom_`. They are therefore reserved as a fixed term beside the direct share (the static_assert above), not counted in a client-driven budget.

### Unchanged

- D8b Lost / Found / Progress / Held / Released semantics.
- D8c credits, routed cap, band and expiry. Routed answers carry `credit` and never `direct`.
- The ABORT for a held direct session behaves as before.

## Concerns / follow-ups

- **No independent review.** The brief said not to spawn subagents, so the usual Opus review was not run. Recommend one before merge.
- **Per-frame hash probe** on the direct path. Before, it was one branch when nothing was held. The sanity runs show no measurable cost, but this is not a micro-benchmark.
- **Expiry depends on the runtime closing the victim.** If a close never arrives, no further expiry happens. That costs liveness, not safety: the caps still hold. The gateway's handler covers both accepted and pending-connect sessions.
- **No NodeConfig knob** for the per-session direct budget: `Worker::setDirectSessionAckBudget` exists, but nothing is wired to it. Add a knob if operators need one.
- **Tag wrap.** `directTag` is `uint16`, as in D8c's `creditTagOn`. A wrapped tag can only make a session look like a reader, which spares it from expiry. That is the conservative direction.
- **A full share of non-held sessions is never expired.** For example, slow readers, or acks=all parked on a slow HW. Direct intake then waits for their progress. This is by design, the same as D8c.
- **The two real-driver tests from D8b** still run on the legacy transport (D8c LOW-5). Not touched here.
- **Planka was unreachable this session** (MCP connect timeout), so no ticket was moved. rune-live pushes were not made either.

# Fix round (review mediums)

This round answers `2026-10-04-d8d-review.md`: APPROVE WITH CHANGES, 0 CRITICAL, 0 MAJOR, 2 MEDIUM and 4 LOW.

- Commit **`609ad26`** on `fix/d8d-direct-path` (wt42). Not pushed.
- Per the coordinator, there is no re-review.

## MEDIUM-1: held sessions parking the band are now expired

**Problem.** At the 4096 fragment limit, 3 stalled sessions (2 × 4096 + 256) park the direct share in its congested band without filling it. An episode started only on a full share, so nothing was expired. Every healthy session with 64 or more in flight was then capped at 64 until the held reaper ran, about 10 minutes later.

**Fix.**
- A band deferral of a non-held session (`mine ≥ 64`) now counts as a deferred pass, just as a full-share deferral does.
- After 64 such passes the held, delivered session with the largest share is expired. All of D8d's guards still apply: held only, never a reader, one per episode, one in flight.

**A second change the test exposed: reader marks are now per scan window.**
- In a long band episode, the stalled sessions had claimed acks early in the episode, while they were still being admitted. That marked them as readers for the rest of the episode, so none was ever expired.
- Now a scan that finds no victim starts a fresh tag. "Reader" therefore means "had an ack claimed in the last 64 deferred passes".
- The gateway's D8c credit tag is unchanged; it still lasts per episode.

## MEDIUM-2: a closed session's buffered frames are dropped before the append

- `SessionAckSink::close` records the session id in a 1024-entry tombstone ring.
- `admitDirect` checks that ring only for a frame whose session is not in the table, which is a cold path. A frame of a tombstoned session returns `kDirectDropClosed`.
- The worker then consumes that frame (CONTINUE) without appending or answering it. This mirrors the gateway's UnknownSession drop.
- A session that was never seen is still admitted (I1: its announcement may not be applied yet). A re-announced session is in the table again, so it is admitted normally.
- This covers every kind of close: expiry, lease reap, Failed and Disconnect.
- Remaining limit: if more than 1024 sessions close before a closed client's image drains, the oldest tombstone is overwritten and that session's leftover frames would be appended. An image drains within a few passes, so this needs an extreme close burst.
