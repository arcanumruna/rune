## Verdict on safety (question 4)

**Still safe.** Neither round-2 change can produce two committing leaders, and no node can follow below its floor.

- **Catch-up** (`onPreVoteResponse`, Election.cpp:1389-1397). It only raises `candidateTermId_`, through
  `proposeMaxCandidateTermId`. The floor, `votedTermId_` and `leadershipTermId_` are unchanged. A higher candidate
  term only removes grants (`term > candidateTermId_`). The D24 relaxed CANVASS gate still lets the node follow a
  live leader at a term ≥ its floor, so a raised candidate term does not strand it.
- **AckTerm.** `ackTerm` ignores the candidate term (AckTerm.h:26), so catch-up cannot freeze the live leader's
  internal-ISR acks.
- **MEDIUM-4 (candidate term only).** The stepped-down leader L can now follow a leader M of term t, with
  T ≤ t < f. This is safe:
  - L never granted, led or followed anything above T.
  - Any leader of f or higher was elected by a majority whose floors are ≥ f. That majority refuses M.
  - So M's majority cannot exist alongside a committing f-leader (majority intersection).
- **Unanimous skip.** The notice-driven StandDown makes followers skip L in `isUnanimousLeader` and
  `isUnanimousCandidate`. Both still require `quorumThreshold` For votes (ClusterMember.h:235, 301). L's ignored
  Against is a liveness signal, not a safety rule: a For-majority intersects every commit majority.
- **MEDIUM-1.** It only adds denials.

## MEDIUM

### MEDIUM-1: The pre-ballot wins on a popcount, but the real ballot loses on any single Against. Round 2's MEDIUM-1 turned lease-holding non-CLOSED nodes and leader-state nodes into vetoes, so a won pre-ballot can now predict a lost real ballot that still bumps durable terms (Election.cpp:824-829, 1356-1360; ClusterMember.h:240-249)

**The two rules disagree:**

- **`preBallotWon_`** counts grants: `popcount >= quorum`. Denials do not matter.
- **`isQuorumLeader`** (Aeron) fails on ANY `Vote::Against` at the candidate's term. `isUnanimousLeader` also fails
  unless the denier is the graceful id. The candidate then times out, after `electionTimeoutNs_`, back to CANVASS.

**What round 2 changed.** Before round 2:

- Non-CLOSED nodes that heard a leader *granted* the real vote.
- Leader-state nodes (`LeaderLogReplication`..`LeaderReady`, `leaderLeaseLive_` true when `selfLeads`) were
  *silent* for a candidate that passed the log and gate checks: no branch placed a vote.

Now both send an explicit **Against**.

**Failure shape.** A candidate wins its pre-ballot with a majority while a minority D still holds a live lease.
That is a legitimate state: L reaches only D, or D processed L's last heartbeats late. Then:

1. The candidate raises its **durable** candidate term and sends RequestVote.
2. The majority grants and raises **their durable floors** to t.
3. D sends Against, so `isQuorumLeader` is false, and the ballot is lost after a full election timeout.
4. The candidate re-canvasses: ≥ 1 more timeout, plus up to 0.5 for nomination.

The process repeats until D's lease expires.

**How long it lasts:**

- In the steady-state shapes it is bounded by about H after D last heard L. L is dying of CheckQuorum, since it
  reaches only a minority.
- Each wasted round costs about 2.5 election timeouts and a durable term bump.
- The grantors' raised floors make them refuse L (and send the notice). That is harmless here, because L is
  already sub-quorum.
- It is not a wedge, but it is exactly the "binding ballot that cannot succeed" PreVote exists to avoid. It is a
  plausible contributor to the contention timeouts (see "Elections under CPU contention").

**Fix (pick one):**

- **(a) Same rule for the pre-ballot.** Make the pre-ballot use the real-ballot rule. Record denials in a
  `deniedMask`, and stand only when `granted >= quorum && deniedMask == 0`, or once the remaining members can no
  longer deny before the deadline. A lease-holding minority then blocks the pre-ballot (no durable bump) instead
  of the real ballot.
- **(b) Treat lease denials as abstentions.** Have the MEDIUM-1 lease deny return **without placing a vote**. In
  leader states, return without a vote too, as before round 2. The ballot is then counted by `isQuorumLeader`
  over the grantors.
  - This is the closer match to etcd ("ignore MsgVote in lease").
  - The ratchet stays closed: the voter still does not grant.

**Test:** 5 nodes, 3 nodes that have not heard L, and 1 node D that has heard L in the last H. Assert that the
first real ballot after a won pre-ballot is won, or that no durable candidate term is raised for a ballot that is
then lost.

### MEDIUM-2: Catch-up discards the grants already collected and keeps the original pre-ballot deadline. A late higher-term denial turns a won (or nearly won) pre-ballot into a lost one (Election.cpp:1389-1397, 410-415)

**What happens:**

- `onPreVoteResponse` rebases `preBallot_.term` and clears `grantedMask` to the self bit. It does this even when
  the pre-ballot was already won but `nominate()` has not run yet: a grant and the denial arrived in the same
  drain.
- It does not move `preBallot_.deadlineNs` (and it has no `nowNs` to do so). The re-ask round must complete in
  what is left of the original `electionTimeoutNs_`.
- Under delayed processing, the higher-term denial is often the *last* response to arrive. The re-asked grants
  then miss the deadline, so the pre-ballot is lost. That costs about 2.5 election timeouts before the next
  attempt, which itself starts with the right term.

**What it does not cause:**

- It is not a loop. Each catch-up strictly raises the term to a value a live member already holds durably, so the
  number of catch-ups is bounded by the number of distinct higher terms.
- It is not inflation. A cut-off node's own pre-votes never raise anyone. Only durable terms that a responder
  already holds propagate, and only as *candidate* terms, which the D24 relaxed gate tolerates.

**Fix:** pass `nowNs` into `onPreVoteResponse`. On a catch-up, set `preBallot_.deadlineNs = nowNs +
electionTimeoutNs_`. This is bounded because catch-ups are bounded.

### MEDIUM-3: The MAJOR-2 lease filter is inert on a restarted node. Production always constructs `Election` with `leadershipTermId = 0`, so a restarted survivor stamps the lease from a superseded zombie again (Election.cpp:1307; BrokerNode.cpp:632-633)

**What happens:**

- `noteLeaderHeard_` rejects only `term < leadershipTermId_`. After a restart, `leadershipTermId_` is 0 until the
  node follows a leader again. The durable floor is restored, but the filter cannot use the floor: the V case
  needs the lease from a below-floor leader.
- In round-1 MAJOR-2's 5-node scenario (Z stuck in `LeaderLogReplication` at T, its outbound up and its inbound
  down; N at T+1 dies), one crash-restarted survivor C does the following:
  - It canvasses.
  - It stamps Z's lease every 50 ms.
  - It sends TermFloorNotice to Z, which never arrives (Z's inbound is down).
  - It denies every pre-vote and every real vote.
- With 3 of 5 alive, the cluster then needs C, so it stays headless for as long as the one-way link lasts.
- `SimCluster::boot(i, T)` passes the restored term. It therefore masks this, because production passes 0.

**Fix:** the root cause is LOW-6. Leader states need a CheckQuorum-style timeout, so a zombie stuck in a leader
state stops heartbeating after H. Until then, either:

- restore `leadershipTermId_` from durable state at init (TermLog or the floor is not the right source; it needs
  the followed term), or
- cap a below-floor lease to one heartbeat timeout after the first notice sent to that leader.

**Test:** a SimCluster test with a restart and production-style `boot(i, 0)`.

### MEDIUM-4: The harness still cannot express the contention timeouts. There is no delivery latency, no per-node processing stall and no "PreVote OFF is RED" sim (ElectionLogTest.cpp:2937-3000)

- MEDIUM-1 and MEDIUM-2 above only show up when responses arrive in different ticks: late, reordered, or with
  one node's drain stalled.
- `SimCluster::step()` delivers everything in the same tick, in node order.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a per-message delivery delay (0-5 ms, seeded) and a per-node "stall for k ms" knob that queues inbound
    messages and processes them with the stall-end `now`.
  - Re-run the convergence sims (8 seeds is fine) with them.
  - Assert election-latency bounds: for example, a leader within H + 3 election timeouts after a kill.

## Elections under CPU contention (the 8 multi-node integration timeouts)

There are code reasons that elections got slower under contention. Every one of them is bounded, and none
explains a timeout on its own, but they compound:

1. **The wider stand window turns more nominations into split votes**, and in Aeron a split is a full lost
   round.
   - Before D29, the first node to pass its nomination deadline stood immediately. Peers still in NOMINATE
     granted it and dropped their own candidacy.
   - Now there is a pre-ballot RTT between the deadline and standing. Under contention that RTT is the drain
     delay of two duty cycles.
   - Nodes in their own pre-ballot grant each other's pre-votes at the same term (`t > candidateTermId_` for
     both). So two pre-ballots that overlap both win, and both stand at t.
   - Each voter grants the first and sends Against to the second. With `isQuorumLeader`'s any-Against rule,
     **both** lose after a full election timeout, then canvass again (≥ 1 timeout) and wait the nomination delay.
   - **Fix:** a node in NOMINATE that grants a pre-vote for term t should push its own nomination deadline back
     (etcd randomises the timeout). At the least, it should not start its own pre-ballot at the same t within
     one status interval of granting.
2. **MEDIUM-1:** a won pre-ballot followed by a real ballot vetoed by one late lease holder.
3. **MEDIUM-2:** a late higher-term denial resets the grants without extending the deadline.
4. **The lease is stamped at processing time, not send time** (`noteLeaderHeard_(…, nowNs)`).
   - A node whose drain stalled processes the dead leader's queued NLTs late. That extends its lease by the stall
     length. Its follower-liveness clock is extended the same way (pre-existing).
   - Peers that timed out on time then lose a pre-ballot to it (3-node: always).
   - This only matters if the stall exceeds about one election timeout. That is plausible with 8 multi-node
     tests in parallel on the Mac.

Each lost round costs about 2.5 election timeouts plus a durable term bump. The tests pass alone and at -j8 when
rerun, which is consistent with these probabilistic costs rather than a wedge. To confirm cheaply: run with
`RUNE_CTRL_LOG=1` and count `pre-ballot without a majority` and `timeout without quorum` transitions on a failing
run.

## Questions 1-3

- **(1) Catch-up.** No inflation, no re-ask loop, and no bad interplay:
  - **Inflation:** only terms a responder already holds durably are adopted, and only as the candidate term. A
    cut-off node's inflated candidate term reaches others only as a denial answer. They adopt it as a candidate
    term, and the D24 relaxed gate lets them follow the live leader anyway.
  - **Follow floor:** untouched.
  - **AckTerm:** ignores the candidate term.
  - **D24 relaxed CANVASS gate:** works, because NOMINATE counts as canvassing.
  - **Remaining issue:** the deadline (MEDIUM-2).
- **(2) MEDIUM-1.** No wedge found, but there is MEDIUM-1 (vetoes). The cases checked:
  - **After a notice step-down,** the notifier V keeps L's lease for up to H (V is not CLOSED, so StandDown is
    ignored). V's vote is not needed:
    - 3 nodes: A wins with L's grant.
    - 5 nodes: A wins with L, B and X.
    - 2 nodes: only L stands, and L is exempt.
  - **Old-version candidates** are denied in lease, as intended.
  - **A leader alive but cut from the majority** loses CheckQuorum within about H, so the lease ends within
    about 2H.
  - **A leader-state zombie** is the LOW-6 or MEDIUM-3 case.
- **(3) MEDIUM-4.** L's re-candidacy is never blocked:
  - `resigned_` is unset, so L canvasses and nominates.
  - Followers dropped the lease and `leaderMember_`, and the notifier exempts L (`leaderHeardId_ == L`).
  - `gracefulClosedLeaderId_ = L` only lets followers nominate without L's canvass position and win
    unanimously without L's vote. It never stops them from granting L.
  - **When L is the only electable node,** the followers' pre-ballots fail on the gate without side effects, and
    L wins by the quorum-candidate path after about 1.5-2 election timeouts.
  - **2 nodes:** the notifier is the only follower and is not CLOSED, so the StandDown is a no-op. L and V elect
    normally, with catch-up from V's floor.
  - The one gap is NEW LOW-1.

## LOW

- **LOW-1: the notice-driven StandDown is sent once and never re-sent** (Election.cpp:951-952). `resigned_` is not
  set, so the `canvass()` re-send loop does not apply. A lost StandDown leaves that follower CLOSED until its
  heartbeat timeout. Meanwhile L cannot reach NOMINATE in 5 nodes: CLOSED followers are silent, so there is no
  canvass quorum. The cost is about H + 2 election timeouts, as in round-1 MEDIUM-3. Fix: re-send it on the status
  interval while L canvasses, until it leaves CANVASS, keyed by a `noticeStepDownTermId_`.
- **LOW-2: in leader states, MEDIUM-1 also removed Aeron's deny-with-raise for a log or gate denial.** The lease
  branch now precedes it. The effect is harmless (the candidate catches up via pre-vote responses), but it is an
  undocumented divergence. Merge it into the MEDIUM-1 fix.
- **LOW-3: `sendPreVoteRequests_` computes `1u << i` with no `i < 32` guard** (Election.cpp:1344).
  `markPreVote_` and `selfBit_` have the guard. Clusters are capped at 16 members
  (`CandidacyState::kMaxMembers`), so this is latent UB only. Add the same guard, or a `static_assert` on the
  member cap.
- **LOW-4: the round-1 LOWs are still open:** LOW-1 (self-vote gated once), LOW-2 (`nextDeadlineNs`, test-only)
  and LOW-3 (the stale pre-ballot term after a deny-with-raise in NOMINATE).
- **LOW-5: spec drift.** `specs/2026-10-08-prevote-design.md` is out of date in four places:
  - It still describes a `PreCandidate` state.
  - It describes `PreVoteResponse{term, voterId, granted}`, without `voterTermId` or the catch-up rule.
  - It says (line 114) that "a notice step-down does not broadcast StandDown", which the code now contradicts.
    Line 76 says it does.
  - It omits the MEDIUM-1 real-vote lease rule, the MAJOR-2 term filter and the MEDIUM-4 "candidate term only"
    rule.
- **LOW-6: the CRITICAL-1 regression boots Y with `leadershipTermId = T`.** Production uses 0. That is fine for this
  test, but see MEDIUM-3: use production-style boots in at least one restart sim.
- **LOW-7: there is still no sim that is RED with PreVote OFF** for the "voter not CLOSED when the RequestVote
  arrives" window. The author's unit-level coverage is accepted. Close it with MEDIUM-4's latency knob: delay
  L→V, so V is canvassing when X's RequestVote lands.
