`specs/2026-10-08-prevote-design.md` and `briefs/2026-10-08-d24r2-d28-review.md` (P1-MAJOR-1).

## Verdict on safety

**No safety violation found.** Neither PreVote, the notice, nor `ownLeaderAsks` can let two leaders
commit in overlapping terms, or let a node follow below its floor:

- **PreVote** (`onPreVoteRequest`, Election.cpp:1347) touches no durable state.
- **Vote-once-per-term** is unchanged. A real grant still raises `candidateTermId_` and the floor
  durably before `placeVote` (Election.cpp:839-841).
- **The notice** only ever makes a leader step down to a term at or above a floor that a member really
  holds. `leaveClosedForElection_` / `raiseFollowFloor_` raise the floor before any later ballot.
- **`ownLeaderAsks`** (Election.cpp:807) only removes *stickiness*, which is a liveness and disruption
  guard, not a safety rule. The follower still runs the full Aeron vote rule: term above its own,
  log/gate, and a durable grant.
- **Zombie leader asking its old followers.** Say Z's followers moved on to N, and a follower F still
  hears Z. F's grant to Z can only matter if Z wins a pre-ballot majority. Every member that hears N
  denies Z, because the lease is held by N and Z ≠ N. So Z cannot collect a majority, and a binding
  RequestVote from Z never reaches F's real-vote path while N lives.
- **Conclusion.** Exempting the followed leader is safe. It can only pull F out of following a zombie,
  which is desirable.

The findings below are about liveness, plus how well the tests prove the change.

## CRITICAL

### CRITICAL-1: A pre-vote denial never tells the candidate the voter's higher term. Aeron's only term catch-up is suppressed, and a 2-of-3-alive cluster can wedge headless forever (Election.cpp:1314-1367, ConsensusMessages.h:789-820)

**Mechanism:**

- **The pre-ballot term is always `candidateTermId_ + 1`** (startPreBallot_, 1318).
- **Voters deny if `term <= candidateTermId_`** (1352).
- **The response carries only `{term, voterId, granted}`.** Nothing in the pre-ballot path ever raises
  the candidate's term.
- **How Aeron catches up.** Consider a node whose log or gate is preferred but whose term is behind.
  In Aeron (and in Rune before D29) it learns the higher term from the *stale* node's **real**
  RequestVote. The voter takes the "our log is fresher / `!mayVoteFor`" deny branch, which does
  `proposeMaxCandidateTermId(candidateTermId)` (Election.cpp:823-832). Its next candidacy is then above
  everyone else's.
- **What PreVote changes.** A stale node now never gets past its pre-ballot, so that real RequestVote
  is never sent and the catch-up never happens.
- **Production reachability.** In production `logLeadershipTermId` and `appendPosition` are always 0
  (BrokerNode.cpp:630-645, 717-731), so `compareLog` never discriminates. The discriminator is the
  CRITICAL-R3 vote gate (`mayVoteFor`, the internal-ISR rule). The same wedge appears with
  `compareLog` once Phase-3 positions are live.

**Failure scenario (3 nodes F, X, Y; a crash/pause sequence the DR suite exercises):**

1. Leader F (term T) is paused (SIGSTOP or GC) for longer than H = `leaderHeartbeatTimeoutNs_`. X and
   Y time out and canvass.
2. Y wins its pre-ballot at T+1: X grants, since it has no lease. `proposeMaxCandidateTermId` gives
   **Y.cand = T+1, durably**. Y is then killed, or X is in INIT or restarting, so Y's RequestVote never
   reaches X. **X.cand stays T.**
3. F resumes. It is still quorate on the AppendPositions sent just before X and Y left CLOSED, so it
   keeps leading T.
   - X re-follows F (T == X.cand).
   - Y restarts. Its floor is ≤ T, so the D24 relaxation lets it follow F.
   - Y is behind, and the leader commits an internal-ISR shrink to {F, X}. Equivalently, with live
     positions, Y simply lags.
4. F dies. The two survivors cannot elect:
   - **X's pre-ballot at T+1:** Y denies, because T+1 ≤ Y.cand (T+1). F is dead. X has 1 of 3 votes
     and loses.
   - **Y's pre-ballot at T+2:** X denies, because `mayVoteFor(Y)` is false (or X's log is fresher).
     Y has 1 of 3 votes and loses.
   - Neither pre-ballot changes any state, so this repeats **forever**. A majority is alive, but the
     cluster has no head until F returns. This violates the time-independent liveness rule.
   - Before D29, Y's real RequestVote at T+2 made X adopt T+2 (deny branch, 830). X then stood at T+3,
     Y granted, and the cluster converged.

The same shape occurs in 2-node clusters, and in 5-node clusters with any term/eligibility inversion.
It also occurs after the D29 upgrade on clusters whose pre-D29 terms were inflated by cut-off nodes
(the classic Raft disruptive-node state).

**Fix (etcd's rule: a rejected pre-vote carries the voter's term):**

- Add `voterTermId = max(candidateTermId_, followFloorTermId_)` to `PreVoteResponse`. This makes the
  blockLength 24, which matches the spec.
- In `onPreVoteResponse`, when `!granted && voterTermId >= preBallot_.term`, do
  `candidateTermId_ = nodeStateFile_.proposeMaxCandidateTermId(voterTermId, …)` (do NOT raise the
  floor). Then restart the pre-ballot at `candidateTermId_ + 1`, either now or on the next nomination.
- This adopts a term that a live member already holds durably. That is not inflation, because a
  cut-off node still cannot create new terms.
- **Test:** a SimCluster with a per-node `setVoteGate`, or distinct `appendPosition`s in `boot()`.
  Reproduce the 4 steps and assert that a leader is elected among X and Y. The test must be RED on
  the current diff.

## MAJOR

### MAJOR-1: StandDown hand-off now waits out the lease. `onStandDown` keeps the resigned leader's lease, so every other follower denies the successor's pre-ballot for H (Election.cpp:1180-1190, 1300-1312)

**Mechanism:**

- `onStandDown` drops the node to CANVASS "election now". It does not clear `leaderHeardId_` or
  `leaderHeardNs_`.
- Every follower heard L's NLT within one status interval. So `leaderLeaseLive_(now, A)` is true for
  any candidate A ≠ L, for H after L's last heartbeat.

**Failure scenario:**

- **5 nodes:** the successor collects only itself plus the resigned L (L grants, since its
  `leaderMember_` is nullptr) = 2 of 5. It loses the pre-ballot after `electionTimeoutNs_`, goes back
  to CANVASS, waits another election timeout plus the nomination delay, and only then wins.
  - The write outage on every rolling restart grows from about 0.5 election timeouts to about 2-2.5
    election timeouts.
  - The skip-the-closed-leader unanimous path (the whole point of controlled shutdown) is defeated.
- **3 nodes:** the hand-off still works, but only because the resigned leader votes. If it has already
  exited (shutdown deadline) or its responses are lost, the 3-node hand-off is delayed the same way.
- **Existing test:** `ElectionStandDownRegression.FollowerElectsAtOnce…` (ElectionLogTest.cpp:2041)
  stops at `Nominate`, so it does not catch this.
- **Spec divergence:** the spec promised the opposite ("followers drop their leader lease at once").

**Fix:**

- In `onStandDown`, after the existing validity checks, clear the lease if
  `leaderHeardId_ == leaderMemberIdArg`: set `leaderHeardId_ = -1` and `leaderHeardNs_ = INT64_MIN`.
- Optionally treat `gracefulClosedLeaderId_` like the lease exemption.
- **Test:** a 5-node SimCluster with `resign()`. Assert that a new leader is CLOSED within about one
  election timeout and that `preBallotsLost == 0`.

### MAJOR-2: The lease is stamped by NLT/CommitPosition at ANY term, including one provably superseded by our own leadershipTermId. A zombie of an older term can then hold every survivor's lease indefinitely (Election.cpp:899 + 983-985, 1115, 1293-1298)

**Mechanism:**

- `noteLeaderHeard_` runs before every gate. That includes the stale-term return at 983
  (`leadershipTermIdArg < leadershipTermId_`) and lower-term CommitPositions.
- The design wanted "heard, even one we refused because of our floor". That case only needs terms ≥
  our `leadershipTermId_`: V's `leadershipTermId_` stays ≤ T, because a grant does not move it. It
  does not need terms below a leadership we have already followed or adopted.

**How a zombie can outlive the lease:**

- A CLOSED zombie self-terminates through CheckQuorum (1269), so its lease is bounded by about 2H.
- A zombie stuck in a **leader state** is not bounded. LeaderLogReplication and LeaderReady have no
  quorum timeout (pre-existing). Such a zombie keeps sending NLT(T) forever and steps down only on a
  higher-term NLT or CommitPosition.

**Failure scenario (5 nodes; one-way partition plus one crash):**

1. Z wins term T. Its inbound links break before any AppendPosition arrives, so Z sits in
   LeaderLogReplication.
2. Z's outbound is also down for a while. The other four elect N at T+1 and follow it. The N→Z link
   stays down, so Z never sees T+1.
3. Z's outbound heals, so NLT(T) reaches everyone every status interval. CLOSED followers of N ignore
   it but **stamp the lease from Z**.
4. N dies. The followers time out and canvass. Every pre-ballot is denied: the lease is held by Z,
   which is refreshed every 50 ms. The notice is never sent, because the stale-term return at 983
   precedes it.
   - With 3 of 5 alive and healthy, the cluster stays headless for as long as the one-way link
     persists.
   - Before D29 they elected among {A, B, C}.

**Fix:**

- Stamp the lease only when `leadershipTermIdArg >= leadershipTermId_`, i.e. the sender is not
  provably superseded from our point of view. Apply this to both the NLT and the CommitPosition sites.
- This keeps the V case (it refused L only by floor), and P1-MAJOR-1 stays closed.
- Separately backlog the leader-state quorum timeout (see LOW-6).

### MAJOR-3: The PreVote tests do not discriminate. No sim test is RED with PreVote disabled, and the harness cannot express the scenarios that matter (ElectionLogTest.cpp:2884-3175, 143-152)

**Answering the author's reasoning: "still pass without PreVote" shows the tests never exercise PreVote, not that the windows are closed.**

- **5-node tests (`CutOffCandidate…`, `NoticeUnderPartialPartition…`).** X (cut from L) can only reach
  NOMINATE with a canvass quorum: `isQuorumCandidate` needs a non-null `logPosition`, i.e. a
  CanvassPosition, from 3 members (ClusterMember.h:259-315). CLOSED followers are silent, so X plus
  one canvassing V is 2 of 5.
  - X **never stands**. In `NoticeUnderPartialPartition…` it does not stand until L itself has
    stepped down.
  - Neither PreVote nor the ping-pong mechanism is exercised.
- **3-node `RestartWhileAPeerIsCutOff…`.** The outcome is decided by a race that the sim always
  resolves one way: zero latency, delivery in the same tick, L's heartbeat every 50 ms. The restarted
  V follows L long before X's nomination deadline, and from then on CLOSED stickiness denies X's real
  RequestVote.
- **Where PreVote is actually load-bearing.** It matters when ≥ quorum−1 *non-sticky* nodes canvass
  while the leader is healthy. For example: 5 nodes with two floored or flapping bridges (X+V+W form a
  canvass quorum, A and B are sticky). Another example: the voter is not CLOSED when the RequestVote
  arrives (L→V delayed or briefly cut). None of the tests builds such a state.
- **On "provably bounded".** The bound actually holds only per real outage: each notice step-down
  needs a fresh period in which a majority did not hear the leader for H. It also depends on
  MEDIUM-1 (real votes ignore the lease) and LOW-5 (mixed versions skip the pre-ballot).

**Harness fidelity gaps:**

- Every node has `appendPosition 0` and `logLeadershipTermId 0`, and there is no vote gate. So
  `compareLog` and `mayVoteFor` never discriminate, and CRITICAL-1 cannot be expressed.
- Delivery is zero-latency, in the same tick, in node-index order, with no jitter or reordering.
- There is one fixed seed per test.
- `boot()` reuses the in-memory `NodeStateFile`/`TermLog` objects instead of reopening the files, so
  durability across a restart is not exercised.
- `doWorkThroughPreBallot` force-grants from all 16 ids. It was threaded into the CRITICAL-R3 gate
  regressions (`VoteGate_StaleSelfKnowledgeStillStands`, `SelfVoteGatedByOwnKnowledge…`,
  ElectionBallotTest.cpp:1017/1070), which therefore no longer test the gate together with the
  pre-ballot. A stale-self-knowledge node now needs a majority of *others* to pre-grant, and nothing
  checks that.

**Assertion problems:**

- `EXPECT_GE(... + (L2 == L ? 0u : 0u), 1u)` (StrandedMember) is a no-op expression.
- `EXPECT_LE(T2, T + 6)` is loose enough to admit two deposals.
- `DeadLeaderIsReplaced…` cuts both directions, so the "zombie heartbeats reach some nodes" bound is
  never measured.

**Fix: add tests that are RED on the right axis:**

- **(a)** PreVote OFF → ping-pong or a deposed healthy leader: 5 nodes, X–L cut, V and W both
  canvassing (floored or restarted), A and B sticky.
- **(b)** CRITICAL-1's inversion (needs per-node gates or positions).
- **(c)** The MAJOR-1 StandDown hand-off.
- **(d)** The MAJOR-2 stale zombie.
- **(e)** A one-way cut where followers no longer reach L: assert a new leader within about
  2H + 2 election timeouts.
- **(f)** A flapping L→V link (on/off with a period below H, for 40 election timeouts): at most one
  leader change, and V's floor never above the live term.
- **(g)** A 2-node pair.
- **(h)** Seed sweeps (≥ 50 seeds) and 0-5 ms random delivery latency in `SimNet`.
- Keep `doWorkThroughPreBallot` for the mechanical rigs only. The gate regressions should drive the
  pre-ballot with gated peers.

## MEDIUM

### MEDIUM-1: The real RequestVote outside CLOSED ignores the lease, so a pre-ballot result can go stale before the binding vote (Election.cpp:820-847)

**What happens:**

- Pre-votes are checked at request time. The binding RequestVote can arrive up to the pre-ballot
  deadline (`electionTimeoutNs_`) later.
- If the voter is still in CANVASS, NOMINATE or FOLLOWER_BALLOT at that point, it grants even though
  it has re-heard L in between. Examples: V refusing L by floor, or V canvassing while L's NLT is
  rejected on log.
- Its floor then rises above T, and the notice deposes L. That is exactly the ratchet PreVote was
  meant to remove.
- Old-version nodes skip the pre-ballot entirely (LOW-5).
- etcd ignores MsgVote while in lease in every state, except for leadership transfer.

**Fix:** deny a real RequestVote in non-CLOSED states when `leaderLeaseLive_(now, candidateId)`, but
keep the deny-with-term-raise semantics (Aeron) for log or gate denials.

### MEDIUM-2: During the pre-ballot (up to `electionTimeoutNs_`) the node stops publishing CanvassPosition and CandidacyState, and PreVoteRequests are not preceded by the candidate's knowledge (Election.cpp:409-421, 1326-1333)

**What happens:**

- `nominate()` returns before the canvass-publish branch (439) while the pre-ballot is active.
- Peers that enter CANVASS in that window `resetMembers()` and cannot count this node:
  `willVoteFor` needs a non-null `logPosition`. So they cannot become quorum or unanimous candidates.
- Voters whose knowledge lacks the candidate's CandidacyState may deny via `mayVoteFor`.
  `candidateBallot` sends "our knowledge first" (489) for exactly this reason.
- The result is delays and lost pre-ballots, not a wedge.

**Fix:**

- In `sendPreVoteRequests_`, call `publishCandidacyState_(id)` before each request.
- In the re-send branch, also `publishCanvassPosition()`.
- Alternatively, treat an incoming PreVoteRequest as canvass information: update `member->logPosition`
  and `logLeadershipTermId`.

### MEDIUM-3: The lease exemption is a single last-writer slot. Any other member's NLT or CommitPosition overwrites `leaderHeardId_`, and a leader that stepped down is then denied by its own followers for H (Election.cpp:1293-1312)

**What happens:**

- After a notice step-down, L's re-candidacy relies on `leaderHeardId_ == L`. It could just as well
  rely on `leaderMember_ == L` while CLOSED, as the real-vote path does.
- A stale NLT from another member, or any CommitPosition with a different id, flips the slot.
- In 5 nodes the exemption is also partly moot: L cannot even reach NOMINATE until A and B time out
  of CLOSED and canvass. CLOSED followers are silent, and L+V is not a canvass quorum when X is cut.
  So the step-down costs about H + 2 election timeouts anyway.

**Fix:**

- For a CLOSED follower, exempt `candidate == leaderMember_->id`. Otherwise keep the per-sender stamp,
  combined with MAJOR-2's term filter.
- Consider having the notice step-down broadcast StandDown(T), as the spec said. Followers then
  canvass at once (fast canvass quorum) and drop the lease (with MAJOR-1's fix).

### MEDIUM-4: The leader adopts the notifier's floor as its own floor. That is an undocumented fifth floor source, and it propagates (Election.cpp:1381-1388, 1403-1412)

**What happens:**

- `onTermFloorNotice` → `leaveClosedForElection_(floor)` / `raiseFollowFloor_(floor)`. The leader's
  durable floor becomes f, although no leader of f may exist (f came from a *failed* grant).
- The D24 invariant ("grant, lead, follow, or adopt from a leader") no longer describes the floor.
- L now advertises `leadershipTermId = f` in CanvassPosition. L will itself refuse, and notify, any
  leader in (T, f).
- It is safe (only more conservative), and the bound is the max granted term.

**Fix:** either raise only `candidateTermId_` (proposeMax), which is enough to stand above f and keeps
the floor's meaning, or document notice-adoption as a floor source in the D24 comment and NodeStateFile.
The first is preferred.

## LOW

- **LOW-1: the self-vote is gated only once, at pre-ballot start** (startPreBallot_, 1319).
  CRITICAL-R3 re-gates every tick in `candidateBallot` (456). Knowledge learned during the pre-ballot
  is ignored, which can lose a winnable pre-ballot. Re-evaluate in `nominate()` before
  `preBallotWon_()`: clear or set self's bit.
- **LOW-2: `nextDeadlineNs()` returns a future deadline once the pre-ballot is already won**
  (174-175). The candidacy then waits up to one status interval under a deadline-driven scheduler or
  test. Return `nowNs` / `INT64_MIN` when `preBallotWon_()`.
- **LOW-3: the pre-ballot term goes stale when `candidateTermId_` rises during NOMINATE.** This
  happens through the deny-with-raise branch (830), which keeps NOMINATE. The candidate keeps counting
  grants for the old `preBallot_.term`, then stands at `candidateTermId_ + 1`, which nobody pre-voted.
  Restart the pre-ballot when `candidateTermId_ >= preBallot_.term`.
- **LOW-4: spec drift.** The code makes the pre-ballot a sub-phase, where the spec has a
  `PreCandidate` state. `PreVoteResponse` blockLength is 16, where the spec says 24 (it will be 24
  after CRITICAL-1). The notice step-down does not broadcast StandDown, contrary to the spec. Update
  the spec.
- **LOW-5: wire and mixed-version behaviour.** It is acceptable as documented:
  - Old nodes count templates 60-62 into `ignoredFrameCount_` (adapter default:, ConsensusAdapter.h:193).
    If that counter is alerted on, expect noise.
  - A new-version candidate needs a new-version majority.
  - Old nodes still send real RequestVotes without a pre-ballot, so the floor ratchet plus notice can
    recur *during* a rolling upgrade.
  - State "upgrade all nodes before relying on the notice". Optionally gate notice sending on a
    cluster protocol version.
  - The transport does not filter by template (AeronIngress.h:72-80), and the agent overrides all
    three handlers. The wiring is complete.
- **LOW-6 (pre-existing, interacts with the lease): leader states have no quorum timeout.**
  LeaderLogReplication and LeaderReady have none, and `leaderLeaseLive_` makes them deny every
  pre-vote unconditionally (1305). A node that won a ballot and lost its inbound links blocks
  elections until a higher-term message arrives (before D29 it also never granted real votes).
  Backlog: a CheckQuorum-style timeout in leader states.
- **LOW-7: time-independence.** The lease reuses the same H clock as `FollowerLiveness` and the
  leader's CheckQuorum, and a candidate nominates at least one election timeout after its own liveness
  expiry. So after a real failure, convergence waits a bounded H instead of racing it. That is
  acceptable under the time-independent rule, given CRITICAL-1, MAJOR-1 and MAJOR-2 are fixed: those
  are the unbounded cases. The 2H window in which a CheckQuorum leader still looks quorate while its
  followers already grant is inherent and benign.
